# Suicide remains top cause of death among Korean youth for 14th straight year

- **Source:** Park Ung, *The Korea Times*, May 22, 2026. Reporting on youth statistics released by Korea's Ministry of Gender Equality and Family.
- **URL:** https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/society/20260522/suicide-remains-top-cause-of-death-among-korean-youth-for-14th-straight-year
- **Read on:** 2026-07-30

## Key figures

- **Suicide has been the leading cause of death among Korean youth for 14 consecutive years, since 2011.**
- Deaths among those aged 9 to 24 in 2024: **1,749 total.**
- Leading causes, per 100,000: **suicide 10.9**, safety accidents 3.3, cancer 2.6. Suicide claimed more than three times as many young lives as safety accidents.
- A Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency survey of **54,170 middle and high school students** found **25.7% — more than one in four — reported experiencing feelings of depression in the past year.**
- The 9–24 population is 7.4 million, 14.4% of Korea's total, down 46.5% from four decades ago.

## Other findings in the same release

- 80.4% of students said youth should engage with social and political issues and make their voices heard (girls 83.9%, boys 77.1%).
- 97.3% said men and women should have equal rights in all areas (girls 98.7%, boys 95.9%).
- Students from multicultural backgrounds rose 145% from 82,536 in 2015 to 202,208 in 2025, now 4% of all students.

## Note on a figure you may see elsewhere

Some summaries claim suicide was "60.2% of all deaths" in this age group. That
figure is **not** in this article and we could not verify it. Use the rates
above, which are stated directly.

## Summary

Suicide has been the leading cause of death for Koreans aged 9 to 24 every year
since 2011, at 10.9 per 100,000 — more than three times the rate of accidents.
One in four middle and high school students reports depression, in a country
where the same survey shows those students are unusually willing to speak up on
social issues.
