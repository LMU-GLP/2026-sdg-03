# Mental Health of Adolescents — WHO Fact Sheet

- **Source:** World Health Organization, fact sheet, published/updated September 1, 2025
- **URL:** https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/adolescent-mental-health
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **One in seven 10–19-year-olds experiences a mental disorder**, accounting for **15% of the global burden of disease** in that age group.
- Anxiety disorders: **4.1%** of 10–14-year-olds, **5.3%** of 15–19-year-olds.
- Depression: **1.3%** of 10–14-year-olds, **3.4%** of 15–19-year-olds.
- ADHD: **2.7%** of 10–14-year-olds, **2.2%** of 15–19-year-olds.
- Conduct disorder: **3.3%** of 10–14-year-olds, **1.8%** of 15–19-year-olds.
- Eating disorders: **0.1%** of 10–14-year-olds, **0.4%** of 15–19-year-olds.
- Schizophrenia in 15–19-year-olds: **0.1%**.
- **Suicide is the third leading cause of death among those aged 15–29 years old.**
- Substance use among 15–19-year-olds (2019/2022 data): **22% alcohol use prevalence** globally (2019); cannabis use **5.5%** among adolescents vs. **4.4%** among adults (2022).

## Note on scope

This is a global fact sheet with worldwide figures by age band (10–14 and
15–19). It does not break out Los Angeles, the United States, or South Korea
specifically — those figures come from [the CDC YRBS source](la-youth-mental-health.md)
and [the Korea Times / Ministry of Gender Equality and Family source](korea-youth-suicide.md).
Do not average or combine this global rate with either country-specific
figure; they measure different populations with different methods.

## Summary

WHO's global figures put mental disorder prevalence at one in seven
adolescents worldwide, with depression more than doubling between the
10–14 and 15–19 age bands, and suicide as the third leading cause of death
for 15–29-year-olds globally. These are worldwide averages, not a
Los Angeles or South Korea number.
