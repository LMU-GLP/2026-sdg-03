# Los Angeles / United States

## What we actually have

We do not have a Los Angeles–specific mental health statistic yet. Our one
U.S.-region source, the CDC's [2023 Youth Risk Behavior Survey](../sources/la-youth-mental-health.md),
is explicit that it is national data and does not break out Los Angeles
County: "The YRBS is a nationally representative survey of U.S. high school
students. It does not break out Los Angeles County." Finding a true
LA-specific figure — from LA County Public Health, LAUSD's own surveys, or
the California Healthy Kids Survey — is still an open task for this team.

## National figures (not LA-specific)

From the [2023 YRBS](../sources/la-youth-mental-health.md), 2021 → 2023:

- Students overall reporting persistent sadness or hopelessness: **42% → 40%**
- Female students reporting the same: **57% → 53%**
- Female students who seriously considered attempting suicide: **30% → 27%**
- Hispanic students: persistently sad or hopeless **46% → 42%**; seriously considered suicide **22% → 18%**
- Black students who attempted suicide: **14% → 10%**
- Getting *worse* over the same period: bullied at school **15% → 19%**; missed school due to safety concerns **9% → 13%**; threatened or injured with a weapon at school **7% → 9%**
- **More than three-quarters of students reported frequent social media use**, associated with higher bullying, sadness/hopelessness, and suicide risk.

The CDC also notes: "Experiencing racism was 2–3 times more prevalent among
students from all marginalized racial and ethnic groups compared to White
students." ([source](../sources/la-youth-mental-health.md))

## A program already working in LA

The [Youstar Foundation](../sources/youstar-foundation-warmline.md) runs a
free, Korean-language warmline for Korean American immigrants in LA County,
staffed by nearly 70 volunteer counselors. The organization cites Korean
Americans in LA County as having "the highest suicide rate among Asian
groups" in the county. This is a county-level claim about a specific
immigrant community, not a general LA youth figure, and it uses a different
measure (suicide rate by ethnic group) than the YRBS figures above (self-reported
symptoms by national survey). See [Existing Programs](existing-programs.md)
for more on this model.
