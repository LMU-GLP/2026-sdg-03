# Existing Programs and Services

## Youstar Foundation Warmline (Los Angeles)

Founded by Soh Yun Park and her husband Sang Kyun Park, the
[Youstar Foundation](../sources/youstar-foundation-warmline.md) runs a free
"warmline" — support one tier below a crisis hotline — for Korean-speaking
and Korean American immigrants in LA County.

- Staffed by **nearly 70 volunteer counselors**, most Korean-speaking.
- Hours: **8 a.m. to 9 p.m.**, with a stated plan to reach 24-hour coverage within three years.
- Built around a specific, cited problem: "Korean Americans in Los Angeles
  County experience the highest suicide rate among Asian groups."
  ([source](../sources/youstar-foundation-warmline.md))

Founder Soh Yun Park, on why the community doesn't seek help: "They hide it,
which prevents them from getting help." ([source](../sources/youstar-foundation-warmline.md))

### Why this matters as a model

This is a working example of a low-cost, volunteer-staffed, language-matched
service — not a clinical program and not an app. It's also the one source in
this wiki that sits directly at the intersection of our two candidate
regions: an LA-based service built specifically for a Korean-speaking
population, addressing a gap (language and stigma) that the
[South Korea](south-korea.md) and [Los Angeles](los-angeles.md) pages both
raise in different forms.

## Gaps

We have one example of an existing program. We do not yet have a source on
a South Korea–based program or a technology product (app/platform) actually
deployed at scale. If the team's concept ends up being a digital product,
finding a comparable existing app (Korean or American) would strengthen the
"has this been tried before" case.
