SDG 3 · Good Health and Well-Being

A practice room for the hardest conversation.

In South Korea, suicide has been the leading cause of death for people aged 9–24 for 14 consecutive years running — and more than a quarter of surveyed students already say they're struggling. We built a way to practice reaching out, before a student is in crisis.

10.9 suicide deaths per 100,000 (ages 9–24, 2024) — the leading cause of death, 14 years running Source: korea-youth-suicide.md
25.7% of 54,170 surveyed middle/high school students reported depression in the past year Source: korea-youth-suicide.md

What we're building

A school-delivered VR program for Korean middle and high school students. Using school-owned headsets, a student works through a short, guided simulation of a social scenario that tends to trigger isolation or withdrawal — being excluded, rejected, or ignored — and practices a response in a low-stakes, repeatable setting, instead of facing it for the first time in real life.

This is a rehearsal tool, not a treatment. It's aimed at the same population as the numbers above: the 25.7% of surveyed students already reporting depression, not a hypothetical group. Source: korea-youth-suicide.md

How it works

  1. Student puts on a school-issued headset During a counseling period or class — not something a student needs to own or bring from home.
  2. Guided isolation/withdrawal scenario A short, scripted moment — exclusion, rejection, being ignored — built for rehearsal, not open-ended exposure therapy.
  3. A counselor is present or waiting throughout Every session pairs the technology with a real adult who can step in. Safety layer 1
  4. In-app pause + referral prompt If the session shows signs of distress, it pauses and surfaces a counselor or warmline contact directly — software doesn't wait for a human to notice first. Safety layer 2

Why this, why now

Leading cause of death, ages 9–24, for 14 straight years since 2011 Source: korea-youth-suicide.md
Suicide rate vs. next-leading cause (safety accidents, 3.3/100k) more than 3× Source: korea-youth-suicide.md
Students surveyed who reported depression in the past year 25.7% of 54,170 Source: korea-youth-suicide.md
UN SDG Target 3.4's own indicator measures only mortality — not depression prevalence a real gap Source: un-sdg3-targets.md

What this isn't

  • × Not a diagnostic or clinical tool — it doesn't assess or diagnose depression.
  • × Not a replacement for the counselor relationship — it's meant to bring students into contact with a counselor sooner, not instead of one.
  • × No Korea-based program precedent exists in our research yet — the closest working example we found, the Youstar Foundation's warmline, operates in Los Angeles, not Korea. Source: youstar-foundation-warmline.md
  • × No sourced figure yet on headset cost or Korean school technology budgets — procurement at scale is an open question, not a solved one.
